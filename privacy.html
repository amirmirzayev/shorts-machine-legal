Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Shorts Machine ("the app") is an internal publishing tool operated by Amir Mirzayev ("we"). It has no public users. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we process

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook when we, the account owner, authorise the app. They are stored on our own computer with restricted file permissions and are used only to upload our videos and read upload status.
  • Basic profile information of our own accounts (display name, account id, avatar) returned by the platform APIs, used to confirm which account a token belongs to.
  • Video files, captions and metadata that we create ourselves.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data about viewers, followers or any person other than ourselves.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer data to third parties.
  • We do not use tracking, advertising or analytics services.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until we revoke the app's access in the platform's settings or delete the token files, whichever comes first. Nothing is stored on servers operated by us.
